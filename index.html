Demo data and assumptions

We use the proposal’s reported detour-tolerance distribution to generate synthetic pairs. Trip and walking times are illustrative. The label is feasible only when carrying capacity, pickup overlap, detour tolerance and arrival deadline all pass.

An ordinary least squares model fits rounded point targets generated by an illustrative pricing formula. Its inputs are detour minutes, urgency, item size, time pressure and ride time. The results measure how closely each model learns its synthetic labels. Real commuter behavior and perceived fairness would require a separate study.
